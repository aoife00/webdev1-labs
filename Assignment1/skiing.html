During the skiing season in 2023 I went skiing in Andora.Andora is a small county in between spain and France

This was my first time going skiing. We spent the week going down the slops in Arinsal.

For the week we stayed in a hotel (Princesa Parc) that was just down the road from the chair lifts to bring you up to the slops.

Click here to find out more about the hotel!!

The layout of my day:

  • Wake up about 8 o'clock head down to breakfast
  • Head to the hotel locker room and get our ski equipment ready
  • Then get the chair lift up to the sloops
  • Spend our free hours befor lunch going down the slops
  • After lunch we joined our ski groups
  • At 5pm headed back to the hotel and got ready for dinner
  • Spent the evening relaxing
