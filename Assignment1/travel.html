My travel adventures

I gained a love for travelling from a young age. As soon as myself and my siblings were at an age where we could remember events my parents took us on our first holiday to Italy.

After this holiday I fell in love with travelling, I love exploring new places and cultures.

My Travel Photos

Having been to 9 different countries over the past few years with both freinds and family from skiing in Andora to holidaying in Portugal, going on a camel ride around volanoes in Lanzarote and competing in the law Empire world championship in Chicago I have got to experience many different places.But by far my favourt place has to be Italy.

  • Peschiera

  • Sorrento
  • Amalfi coast (Positano,ravello)
  • Capri

I have many different memories that I have attached to places in Italy which is another reason I love to travel. You go from planning a trip for a while from researching online and creating a plan of places you want to visit to be able to experience it in person.

From seeing learning about the history of Pompeii and climbing mt.Vesuvuius to visiting Capri and taking a cable car trip across the island , then on to Rome where I got to see and learn about all the historic landmarks.It is safe to say I do love to make my trips busy and go exploring instead of laying out by the pool or beach all day.
